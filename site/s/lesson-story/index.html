Meet the stablecoin

Sam does design work for a client in another country. A bank transfer takes days and loses $25 in fees.

The client offers to pay in USDC instead. It arrives in minutes.

A stablecoin is a token built to stay at one price, usually $1. That's what makes it useful for saving and paying.
